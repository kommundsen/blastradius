The ramp is semantic, not absolute: 100 is the faintest tint, 900 the strongest. Dark theme mirrors it, so 600 is the brand fill in both, and interaction always moves one step (hover) then two (press) toward more contrast. --color-accent-ink is the text-safe step; --color-accent-line the border step.
